If the starting free cash flow is negative and continues growing from a negative base, the discounted cash flows remain negative, resulting in a negative enterprise value.



This is actually mathematically correct.



What do professional analysts do?



They generally don't apply a standard DCF to companies with structurally negative FCF. Instead, they might:



Use EBITDA or EBIT-based valuation.

Forecast a turnaround period before cash flow becomes positive.

Use EV/EBITDA or EV/Sales multiples.

Exclude those companies from a DCF screen.



So for your project, I'd recommend handling this explicitly rather than forcing a DCF result.
